eSIM Not Working?

If you've landed and your eSIM won't connect, try these fast fixes:

  1. Turn on Data Roaming: Make sure Data Roaming is turned ON for your eSIM line.
  2. Check Primary Data Line: Ensure your phone is using the eSIM for cellular data, not your primary SIM.
  3. Restart Phone: Sometimes a simple reboot forces the phone to connect to the local network.
  4. Check APN Settings: Some providers require you to manually type in an APN in your settings. Check your provider's instructions.
