How to Install an eSIM

Installing an eSIM is fast and easy. Do it before you leave for the airport!

  1. Buy an eSIM plan online.
  2. Check your email for the QR code.
  3. Go to Settings > Cellular on your phone.
  4. Tap 'Add Cellular Plan' or 'Add eSIM'.
  5. Scan the QR code and follow the on-screen instructions.

Important Tips

Do NOT turn on the new eSIM line until you arrive at your destination to avoid prematurely starting the plan's validity period.
