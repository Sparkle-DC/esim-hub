eSIM vs Roaming

Should you use an eSIM or just pay for international roaming?

Carrier Roaming

Roaming is convenient because you do nothing—just land and use your phone. However, it's often very expensive (e.g., $10-$12/day on US carriers) and can have slow speeds.

Travel eSIM

An eSIM takes 5 minutes to set up but can save you over $100 on a 1-2 week trip. It provides fast local data for a fraction of the cost.

Verdict

If your company pays for your phone, roaming is fine. If it's your own money, get an eSIM.
