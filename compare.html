Frequently Asked Questions

Do all these eSIMs support 5G?

Yes — Saily, Holafly, Airalo, Nomad, and Breeze all offer 5G where the local network supports it.

Can I use hotspot with unlimited plans?

Yes, but most unlimited plans have a daily high-speed hotspot limit (500 MB – 5 GB) then throttle. Check the provider page for exact limits.

Are these plans data-only or do they include calls/SMS?

All are data-only (perfect for WhatsApp, iMessage, etc.). If you need a phone number, choose Airalo or Nomad plans that include it.
