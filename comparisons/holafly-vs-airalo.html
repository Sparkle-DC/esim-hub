Holafly vs Airalo

The classic dilemma: Unlimited Data vs Fixed Budget. Which one should you choose in 2026?

At a Glance

Holafly

Excellent for heavy users who stream, work, or use social media heavily. Offers true unlimited data plans in most major destinations.

  • Unlimited Data on almost all plans
  • Perfect for video streaming and backups
  • Hotspot sharing often restricted

Airalo

Great for budget travellers who know exactly how much data they need. Offers a wider range of cheap, small-data options.

  • Cheaper for short trips or light usage
  • Hotspot sharing is fully supported

The Verdict

Pick Holafly for absolute peace of mind. If you want to use maps, watch YouTube, post on Instagram, and make video calls without ever checking your data balance, the slightly higher upfront cost is totally worth it.

Pick Airalo to save money if you are a light user. If you just need Google Maps and occasional WhatsApp messages, a cheap 3GB or 5GB plan from Airalo will save you a lot of money. It is also the better choice if you absolutely need to share your connection via hotspot.
