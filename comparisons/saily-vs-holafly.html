The Verdict

Pick Saily if you want the absolute best price. For basic needs like checking Google Maps, a few social media updates, and messaging, Saily’s fixed data plans are incredibly cheap and highly reliable.

Pick Holafly if you hate tracking data usage. It’s perfect for digital nomads, heavy streamers, or anyone who just wants to turn on their phone and use it exactly as they would at home without fear of running out of data.
