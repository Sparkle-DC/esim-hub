Best eSIM for Europe (2026)

For Europe, regional coverage matters more than the cheapest single-country plan. If you cross borders, choose a Europe-wide eSIM.

Saily vs HolaflyInstall guideCompare all

Best overall

Saily is a strong value pick for normal travellers who need maps, messaging, rideshare and light browsing across Europe.

Best unlimited option

Holafly is better if you do not want to think about data usage. It costs more, but unlimited data removes stress on longer trips.

Best budget option

Airalo can work well for shorter trips if the GB allowance matches your expected use.

Buying advice

Do not buy a France-only or Italy-only plan if your itinerary includes multiple countries. A regional Europe plan is usually safer.
